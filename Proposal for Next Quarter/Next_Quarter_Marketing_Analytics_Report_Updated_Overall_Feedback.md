## Pass

### 🎉 Congratulations on passing!

Congratulations on successfully completing the project! Your work brings together objective tracking and analysis of audience, marketing, sales, and product categories. You have demonstrated the importance of turning business questions into clear analysis and communicating the results in a way that supports decision-making.

Keep building on these skills by focusing on the following:
- **Answer the question:** Make sure each section directly addresses its stated business question and includes the relevant data.
- **Choose clear visuals:** Use suitable charts with readable titles, labels, and legends so the audience can understand the results quickly.
- **Explain the takeaway:** Describe what each result means, why it matters, and how it relates to the business objective.
- **Connect evidence to decisions:** Make recommendations or conclusions traceable to the analysis rather than leaving the audience to interpret the charts alone.

Keep practicing these skills in future projects. Each analysis is an opportunity to sharpen your analytical thinking and communicate insights more confidently. 🚀

## Fail

### 💪 Good effort — keep improving!

Thank you for the effort you put into this project. Some parts of the analysis or presentation still need attention, but you can improve them step by step. Use the rubric feedback as a guide and work through the gaps one at a time rather than trying to change everything at once.

Prioritize these improvements:
- **Review every question:** Confirm that each required audience, marketing, sales, and product-category question is clearly stated and answered.
- **Check the data:** Make sure the information shown covers the required groups or categories and supports the point being discussed.
- **Improve chart clarity:** Add clear titles, labels, and legends, and choose a visual that makes the comparison or pattern easy to understand.
- **Strengthen the explanation:** Explain what the result shows and why it matters instead of relying on the chart alone.
- **Recheck the full story:** Ensure the objectives, analysis, and conclusions fit together and that each section contributes to the overall presentation.

Make these changes gradually, then review the presentation from the audience’s perspective. Clear, relevant analysis and a well-explained takeaway will make the work stronger. Keep going — improvement comes from reviewing and refining your work. 🌱
